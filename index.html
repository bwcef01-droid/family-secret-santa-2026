The rules of the swap

Your sign-up isn't finished until you accept these.

$25Minimum spend
$40Maximum spend
  1. Stay in the budget. Spend at least $25 and no more than $40 on your gift.
  2. You give one, you get one. Signing up means you buy a gift for the person you draw, and someone else buys one for you.
  3. Keep it secret. Don't tell anyone who you drew, and don't ask who drew you.
  4. Shop from their answers. Use the sizes and favorites your person gave so the gift fits them.
  5. Follow through. If something comes up and you can't take part, tell the organizer before names are drawn so nobody goes without.
